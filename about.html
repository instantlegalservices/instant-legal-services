About ILS

Instant Legal Services

A legal information and professional-network platform designed to make legal research, public legal resources and matter-based professional coordination easier to discover.

What ILS provides

Legal information and research access; searchable judgment records; practice-area guides; access to official public court services; approved public professional profiles; and a structured route for submitting a legal requirement.

How the network works

A requirement can be described with basic matter and jurisdiction details. Where appropriate, ILS coordinates with an independent legal professional based on the nature of the matter, jurisdiction and professional availability.
