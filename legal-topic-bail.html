Bail & Anticipatory Bail: Research Guide

Bail questions depend on the offence, governing statute, stage of proceedings, court jurisdiction, custody status and facts. Use a verified source before relying on any proposition.

Research checklist

  • Identify the current statutory provision and whether special legislation applies.
  • Check the procedural stage and the court competent to grant the relief.
  • Review recent Supreme Court and relevant High Court decisions.
  • Verify any special restrictions, statutory bars, conditions and subsequent amendments.
  • Do not treat an AI summary as a substitute for the original order or case-specific legal advice.
