🎓 ILS kaam seekhiye — bilkul step by step

Yahan simple language mein sikhaya jayega: problem samajhna, form bharna, evidence lena, kaam karna aur sahi jagah forward karna.

1
👂

Pehle dhyan se suno

Customer jo problem bataye, pehle poori baat samjho. Jaldi mein answer mat do.

Yaad rakho:
  • Naam, mobile aur city/district sahi lo.
  • Problem ko apne shabdon mein confirm karo.
  • Jo nahi pata, uska guess mat karo.
2
📝

Form sahi bharo

Form mein wahi information bharo jo customer ne di hai. Spelling aur mobile number dobara check karo.

3-check rule:
  • 1️⃣ Name check
  • 2️⃣ Mobile check
  • 3️⃣ Problem + city check
3
📂

Evidence dhyan se lo

Evidence ka matlab: bill, notice, receipt, screenshot, document ya doosri zaroori file jo customer ke paas ho.

Important:
  • Sirf zaroori evidence lo.
  • File clear hai ya nahi, check karo.
  • Original document apne paas mat rakho.
  • OTP, password, PIN ya bank password kabhi mat maango.
4
🛡️

Fraud se bachna: sach aur discipline

Misrepresentation, misguidance aur greed nahi. Customer ya ILS ko galat baat, jhooti identity, jhootha status, galat information ya false promise kabhi mat do.

Zero-tolerance rules:
  • ❌ Apne role/authority ko badha-chadha kar mat batao.
  • ❌ Customer ko guaranteed result, guaranteed job, guaranteed payment ya guaranteed legal outcome ka lalach mat do.
  • ❌ Customer se extra paisa, personal commission ya personal benefit mat maango.
  • ❌ ILS/client ki information ka misuse, copying ya unauthorized sharing mat karo.
  • ❌ Kisi document, screenshot, receipt ya status ko badal kar/galat tarike se present mat karo.
  • ✅ Doubt ho: STOP → VERIFY → ASK ILS → THEN ACT.
5
🛠️

Assigned kaam hi karo

ILS ne jo kaam diya hai, wahi karo. Customer ko apni taraf se legal result, guarantee ya pakka promise mat do.

Safe working:
  • Official/ILS process follow karo.
  • Confusion ho to rukkar senior/ILS ko pucho.
  • Legal opinion dene ka claim mat karo agar aap authorized professional nahi hain.
6
📤

Sahi jagah forward karo

Kaam complete hone ke baad information/evidence ko ILS ke bataye process mein forward karo.

Forward karne se pehle:
  • Right customer / right matter check.
  • Files complete aur readable check.
  • Galat person ko document mat bhejo.
7
🔐

Account aur client data ki security

ILS ka login, password, OTP, client documents aur private information kisi ke saath share nahi karni.

Security rules:
  • ❌ Apna login/password/OTP kisi ko mat do.
  • ❌ Kisi aur ka login use mat karo.
  • ❌ Client ki photo/document ko personal phone, social media ya personal cloud par unnecessary save/share mat karo.
  • ❌ Suspicious link, payment request ya unusual instruction par turant kaam mat karo.
  • ✅ Doubt ya suspicious activity ho: STOP → evidence preserve karo → ILS ko report karo → further action tabhi karo jab verify ho.
8
✅

Kaam ka record rakho

Jo kaam kiya, kab kiya aur kya forward kiya — ILS ke available process mein record/update karo.

Golden rule: Sach, sahi aur traceable information. Galti ho jaye to chupao nahi — turant batao.

9
🎯

Ab real-life practice karein

Teen chhote examples. Har situation mein safe aur disciplined action choose karein.

Situation 1: Customer bolta hai “mera kaam 100% ho jayega na?”
Situation 2: Customer kehta hai “OTP bata deta hoon, aap kaam kar do.”
Situation 3: Aapko customer ki file kisi unknown WhatsApp number par bhejne ko kaha gaya.
Practice pass: 3 mein se 3 correct hona zaroori hai. Galti ho to practice dobara karein.
10
🚨

Problem / Fraud dikhe to kya karein?

Galti ya suspicious activity ko hide nahi karna. Jaldi report karna hi safe working hai.

Simple reporting rule:
  • 1️⃣ Kaam rok do agar risk/suspicion ho.
  • 2️⃣ Jo relevant evidence hai use preserve karo; delete/edit mat karo.
  • 3️⃣ ILS ko approved reporting channel se batao.
  • 4️⃣ Customer ko unnecessary allegation ya panic message mat bhejo.
  • 5️⃣ Verification ke baad hi next action lo.
Emergency-type immediate danger ho to appropriate official emergency authority ko contact karna priority ho sakta hai.
11
🧠

Training complete — ab final test

Har question dhyan se padho. Sab answers sahi hone par training complete hogi. Galti hone par training dobara karni hogi.

Pass rule: 10 mein se 10 sahi.
1. Customer ki baat samajhne ka pehla step?
2. Form submit karne se pehle kya check karna hai?
3. Customer ka OTP/PIN lena chahiye?
4. Associate ko kis kaam par kaam karna hai?
5. Document forward karne se pehle?
6. Kaam mein galti ho jaye to?
7. Customer ko ILS service ke baare mein kya batana hai?
8. Extra paisa ya personal commission lene ka sahi rule?
