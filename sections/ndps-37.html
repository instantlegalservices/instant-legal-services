NDPS Act, 1985 — Section 37: Cognizable and non-bailable

हिंदी खोज: NDPS Act, 1985 — Section 37: Cognizable and non-bailableEnglish search: NDPS Act, 1985 — Section 37: Cognizable and non-bailable

Statutory position

Section 37 provides that offences under the NDPS Act are cognizable and non-bailable and contains special statutory conditions relevant to bail. The exact application must be assessed with the charged offence, quantity, facts and precedent.
