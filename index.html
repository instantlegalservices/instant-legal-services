About Instant Legal Services

Instant Legal Services is a legal assistance and professional legal network platform established with the objective of facilitating access to appropriate legal professionals across India.

Through a structured network of advocates and legal professionals associated with different jurisdictions, courts and practice areas, the platform aims to connect individuals, families, businesses and organisations with suitable legal assistance based on the nature and location of their legal requirements.

Our network encompasses legal assistance across a wide range of practice areas, including Criminal Law, Bail & Anticipatory Bail, NDPS matters, Cyber Crime, Matrimonial & Family Law, Civil & Property Disputes, Consumer Matters, Banking & Financial Disputes, Cheque Bounce & Negotiable Instruments matters, Motor Vehicle & Accident Claims, Labour & Employment Law, Commercial & Corporate Law, Contractual & Business Disputes, Insolvency & Bankruptcy matters, Taxation & GST-related matters, Intellectual Property Rights, Constitutional & Writ matters, Service Matters, Arbitration & Alternative Dispute Resolution, Real Estate matters, Documentation & Legal Drafting, Court Marriage & Registration, and other specialised areas of law.

Where a matter requires specialised expertise or representation before a particular court, tribunal, authority or forum, the platform facilitates coordination with an appropriate professional from its network, subject to professional availability, jurisdiction, verification and applicable law.

Our Approach

Right Professional  •  Right Jurisdiction  •  Right Legal Assistance

We focus on structured coordination, professional communication, matter-based allocation and efficient client support while maintaining appropriate confidentiality and respecting the professional and ethical obligations applicable to legal practitioners.

Our Vision

Our vision is to develop a reliable, professionally coordinated and technology-enabled legal network across India, making it easier for people to identify and approach suitable legal professionals for their legal requirements.
