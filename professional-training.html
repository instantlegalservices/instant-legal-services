Professional Orientation

🎓 ILS mein professional tareeke se kaam karein

Advocate, CA aur CS network participants ke liye short orientation: client handling, website workflow, discipline, data security aur responsible professional communication.

Yeh training kis ke liye hai?
Apni professional category select karein.
👥 HR-specific work scope

HR participant ko HR-related assigned work hi diya jana chahiye — jaise permitted HR documentation, employee-data/process coordination, recruitment-process support ya other approved HR operational tasks. Legal advice, advocate work ya unauthorized professional representation assignment ka hissa nahi hoga.

Assignment rule: actual work allocation ko verified role + applicable eligibility + approved assignment scope ke basis par server-side process se enforce karna hoga.

1
🤝

Client ko pehle samjhein

Client ki problem, objective aur available information ko dhyan se samjhein. Jaldi mein assumption ya promise na karein.

  • Important facts confirm karein.
  • Jo information verified nahi hai, use fact ke roop mein present na karein.
  • Client ko respectful, clear aur professional language mein guide karein.
2
🌐

ILS website ka basic workflow

Website ko client ke liye simple action path ki tarah use karein.

  1. Problem / requirement samjhein.
  2. Relevant ILS tool, legal information ya assistance route identify karein.
  3. Official source available ho to uski taraf guide karein.
  4. Professional assistance ki zarurat ho to appropriate verified professional route use karein.
  5. Jo action actually hua hai, usi ka status batayein.
3
📝

Case / requirement information accurate rakhein

Website ya ILS process mein information enter karte waqt accuracy aur traceability important hai.

  • Name, mobile, location aur matter details double-check karein.
  • Document/status ko edit karke misleading presentation na karein.
  • Duplicate ya unnecessary entries avoid karein.
  • Galti mile to hide na karein; approved ILS process mein correction/report karein.
4
🛡️

Professionalism & discipline

  • ❌ False promise, guaranteed outcome ya misleading claim nahi.
  • ❌ Client se unauthorized personal fee/commission/payment diversion nahi.
  • ❌ Role, qualification ya authority ko misrepresent nahi karein.
  • ❌ Client/ILS information ka unauthorized copying, sharing ya misuse nahi.
  • ✅ Doubt ho: STOP → VERIFY → ASK ILS → THEN ACT.
5
🔐

Account, documents & privacy

  • Login, password, OTP aur PIN share na karein.
  • Client documents ko unnecessary personal storage/social media par save ya share na karein.
  • Suspicious link, unusual instruction ya unverified payment request par ruk jaayein.
  • Payment/refund/verification ke liye OTP/PIN/password shortcut kabhi use na karein.
  • Security incident ho to relevant evidence preserve karke approved ILS reporting route follow karein.
6
📤

Communication & handoff

Right matter ko right person/process tak clear information ke saath pahunchana important hai.

  • Recipient aur matter verify karein.
  • Required documents/information complete hai ya nahi check karein.
  • Client ko unnecessary internal confusion ya unverified allegation forward na karein.
  • Escalation required ho to ILS ke approved route ka use karein.
7
🚨

Risk, mistake ya fraud dikhe to response

  1. Risk ho to action pause karein.
  2. Relevant evidence preserve karein; delete/edit na karein.
  3. Approved ILS reporting channel se inform karein.
  4. Verification ke baad hi next action lein.
8
🧠

Final understanding check

8 mein se 8 answers sahi hone par orientation check pass hoga.

1. Client guaranteed result poochta hai. Kya karein?
2. Unknown person client file maangta hai.
3. Client OTP share karna chahta hai.
4. Apni qualification/authority ko badha kar batana?
5. Record mein galti milti hai.
6. Unverified payment request aaye.
7. Client data ko personal social media/cloud par unnecessary save karna?
8. Doubtful instruction mile to basic rule?
Important: Yeh orientation professional credential, registration, verification, employment, assignment ya legal qualification ka substitute nahi hai. Actual ILS onboarding/verification applicable eligibility aur ILS review ke according hi hoga.
