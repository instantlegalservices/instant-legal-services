Traffic E-Challan Information & Assistance

Find the appropriate next step for a traffic eChallan. ILS provides independent information and enquiry routing; challan records, payment, compounding and adjudication remain with the official portal and competent authorities.

01. Check eChallan status

Use the official government eChallan portal to look up challan information using the details requested there.

02. Understand challan details

Review the displayed violation, date, amount and available instructions before deciding what to do next.

03. Payment and receipt guidance

Complete any government challan payment only through the official portal or authorised government channel. ILS does not collect challan amounts.

04. Dispute and court-related enquiries

If you wish to contest a challan or need legal guidance, submit an enquiry for possible coordination with an independent advocate.

ILS is an independent assistance platform, not a government department. Enquiry submission does not create a paid order or guarantee a result. Availability, documents, scope and applicable ILS charges are confirmed before work begins. Government fees, taxes and statutory payments are separate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I check or pay a traffic challan?

Use the official Parivahan eChallan portal. Verify the website address before entering vehicle or personal details.

Can ILS cancel or waive my challan?

No. ILS cannot cancel, waive or decide a government challan. Any challenge or relief is subject to the competent authority or court.

Should I share OTPs or payment details with ILS?

No. Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, card details or UPI credentials. Make government payments only on official channels.
