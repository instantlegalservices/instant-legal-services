Money Recovery & Outstanding Dues Assistance

Unpaid invoices, loans and business dues can require different next steps. Identify the type of outstanding amount and the documents available so your enquiry can be routed for suitable assistance.

01. Unpaid business invoices

Outstanding supply bills, invoices, service payments and documented trade dues.

02. Loans and personal advances

Documented personal loans, advances, repayment schedules and payment defaults.

03. Cheque-bounce matters

Section 138 Negotiable Instruments Act-related enquiries and coordination with an independent advocate where appropriate.

04. Demand notices and follow-up

Demand notice drafting enquiries, payment reminders and structured recovery communication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents may help with a recovery enquiry?

Depending on the matter, invoices, agreements, bank records, acknowledgements, delivery proofs, messages and payment history may help. Share only relevant records.

Can ILS guarantee recovery of my money?

No. Recovery depends on evidence, the other party, limitation, applicable law and decisions of competent authorities. No recovery outcome is guaranteed.

Can I enquire about a cheque-bounce matter?

Yes. You can submit the basic details and available cheque/return memo information for an initial routing enquiry. Legal advice and representation are handled by an independent advocate where accepted.
