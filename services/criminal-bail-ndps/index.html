Criminal Law, Bail & NDPS Assistance

Request legal assistance for criminal complaints, police matters, regular or anticipatory bail and NDPS-related proceedings. Criminal matters can involve urgent deadlines and liberty. A qualified advocate must assess the facts and documents before legal strategy or representation is agreed.

Request criminal law assistance →

Available assistance areas

  • Criminal complaint and case assessment
  • Regular or anticipatory bail enquiry
  • NDPS matter and procedural assistance enquiry
  • Court-document and hearing preparation

Documents to keep ready

  • FIR or complaint number and police station, if available
  • Sections mentioned in the FIR or notice
  • Arrest, custody or hearing status and relevant dates
  • Bail orders, remand papers or court notices, if available
  • Name of court and case number, if already filed

How it works

  1. Submit location, case stage and urgency.
  2. ILS checks advocate availability and jurisdiction.
  3. Advocate engagement, fee and scope are confirmed.
  4. Documents and next steps are reviewed with the appointed advocate.

Important information

No bail or case outcome is guaranteed. Courts decide applications on facts, evidence and law. For arrest, custody or imminent deadlines, contact a qualified local advocate immediately; do not wait for an online response. ILS confirms professional availability and fees before engagement. The ₹299 initial enquiry fee, where applicable, is credited against eligible same-matter ILS fees if you proceed; it is not a court or government fee.

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVV or remote-access permissions.
