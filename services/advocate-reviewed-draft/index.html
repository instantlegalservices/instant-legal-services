Advocate-Reviewed Draft

Have an existing draft reviewed by an advocate for clarity, legal issues and corrections within an agreed scope.

A draft prepared through a tool or by the client may need professional review before use. This service provides a review route for an existing draft; the exact depth of review and whether redrafting is included must be confirmed before work begins.

What this service can include

  • Review of the supplied draft
  • Observations on clarity, missing information and legal issues
  • Corrections or suggested changes within the agreed scope
  • Separate quote if substantial redrafting is required

Documents to keep ready

This is a starting checklist. Share only relevant records; the final list depends on the matter, jurisdiction and confirmed scope.

  • Editable draft or readable PDF
  • Purpose and intended recipient of the document
  • Relevant facts and supporting records
  • Jurisdiction and applicable transaction or proceeding
  • Any deadline for issue, filing or execution

How the service works

  1. Upload or describe the draft securely through the approved channel
  2. ILS confirms document type and review scope
  3. Professional fee and turnaround are agreed
  4. Review comments or revised draft are delivered as agreed

Fees, scope & important information

ILS confirms the exact deliverables, professional availability, applicable ILS fee and expected timeline before work begins. Government fees, court fees, stamp duty, portal charges, DSC and third-party costs are separate unless expressly included in a written quote.

Where applicable, the ₹299 initial enquiry fee is credited in full against eligible ILS professional or service charges for the same matter if you proceed. It is not a government fee and does not automatically include the full professional assignment.

No legal or government outcome is guaranteed. Legal advice, certification, audit, filing, representation or statutory sign-off is provided only by an eligible independent professional under a separately agreed engagement. Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVV or remote-access permissions.

Frequently asked questions

Does review include complete redrafting?

Not automatically. Substantial redrafting is separately scoped and quoted.

Can an advocate certify the document?

Certification, attestation or notarisation is not included unless specifically agreed and legally available.
