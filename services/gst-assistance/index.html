Frequently Asked Questions

What information is needed for GST assistance?

The GSTIN or registration status, business type, relevant tax period, notice details if any, and relevant sales/purchase records may be needed. Do not share passwords or OTPs.

Does ILS file every GST return automatically?

No. The requirement and documents are reviewed first. Filing or representation is undertaken only after scope, responsibility and applicable charges are confirmed.

Are government GST payments included in ILS charges?

No. Any statutory tax, interest, late fee or government payment is separate from ILS service charges and should be paid through official government channels.
