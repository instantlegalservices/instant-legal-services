Frequently Asked Questions

What details should I provide for a legal draft?

Explain the purpose, parties, important dates, location and outcome sought. Share only relevant documents and redact sensitive information where possible.

Does ILS guarantee a legal outcome?

No. A draft does not guarantee acceptance, a court result or a particular outcome. The applicable law, facts and professional review determine the final advice and document.

Are fees fixed before I submit a request?

The requirement is reviewed first. Scope, availability, documents and applicable ILS charges are clarified before you decide to proceed.
