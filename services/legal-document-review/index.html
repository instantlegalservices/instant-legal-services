Legal Document Review

Understand key clauses, obligations, dates and potential issues in a supplied legal or commercial document.

Before signing, responding to or relying on a document, it is useful to understand what it says and what may need attention. ILS can coordinate a document-review request with a suitable professional after the document type and scope are identified.

What this service can include

  • Clause and obligation review
  • Key dates, payment and termination terms
  • Potential gaps, ambiguities and risk observations
  • Written summary or marked-up review, as agreed

Documents to keep ready

This is a starting checklist. Share only relevant records; the final list depends on the matter, jurisdiction and confirmed scope.

  • Complete document and annexures
  • Purpose for which the document will be used
  • Names and roles of the parties
  • Related agreement, notice or correspondence, if any
  • Specific concerns or clauses requiring attention

How the service works

  1. Identify the document type and page count
  2. Confirm scope, jurisdiction and professional availability
  3. Agree fee and deliverable format
  4. Receive the review through the agreed channel

Fees, scope & important information

ILS confirms the exact deliverables, professional availability, applicable ILS fee and expected timeline before work begins. Government fees, court fees, stamp duty, portal charges, DSC and third-party costs are separate unless expressly included in a written quote.

Where applicable, the ₹299 initial enquiry fee is credited in full against eligible ILS professional or service charges for the same matter if you proceed. It is not a government fee and does not automatically include the full professional assignment.

No legal or government outcome is guaranteed. Legal advice, certification, audit, filing, representation or statutory sign-off is provided only by an eligible independent professional under a separately agreed engagement. Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVV or remote-access permissions.

Frequently asked questions

Will the review confirm title or authenticity?

No. Verification of title, records or authenticity requires a separate due-diligence scope.

Are government charges included?

No. Applicable statutory, registry and third-party charges are separate.
