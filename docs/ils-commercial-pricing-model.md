# ILS Commercial Pricing & Delivery Guardrails (Internal Draft)

**Status:** Internal planning only — not approved for public display or checkout  
**Prepared:** 28 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Individual GST/ITR services and recurring small-business compliance plans

## 1. Commercial positioning

ILS should compete on **clear scope, local accessibility, transparent pricing and reliable follow-through**, not on the lowest headline price. Every price must be tied to a measurable workload unit: GSTIN, invoice count, return period, entity count, bank accounts, documents or professional review time.

Do not promise tax savings, refund outcomes, notice outcomes, registration approval or legal results. Government/statutory fees, late fees, stamp duty, taxes on ILS fees and third-party charges must be disclosed separately.

| ITR-1 simple salaried | ₹699 | One employer, routine income and standard deductions |
| ITR-2 with capital gains | ₹1,499 onwards | Basic capital-gain statement; high-volume trades quoted separately |

**Pricing logic:** the nil/low-volume offer is an acquisition entry point. Higher-volume tiers rise with data review and reconciliation effort. Do not include full bookkeeping, audit, litigation, appeals or historic clean-up in a low-priced return-filing SKU.

## 4. Proposed recurring business plans

Plans are enquiry-led until delivery costs, terms, staff capacity and recurring billing controls are approved. The monthly fees below are proposed starting points, not live checkout prices.

| Plan | Proposed monthly fee | Included workload (initial pilot cap) | Exclusions |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Shopkeeper Starter | ₹999 | 1 GSTIN; up to 50 invoices/month; GSTR-1 + GSTR-3B; due-date reminders; monthly filing proof and basic summary | Full bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, notices, annual return, payroll, TDS |
| Retailer Plus | ₹1,999 | 1 GSTIN; up to 150 invoices/month; GST returns; basic GSTR-2B mismatch summary; monthly compliance checklist | Supplier chasing, full books, multiple GSTINs, notices, annual return |
| Wholesaler Compliance | ₹3,999 | 1 GSTIN; up to 400 invoices/month; GST returns; monthly purchase-vs-2B reconciliation summary; mismatch list and monthly report | Full accounting, stock audit, e-invoice/e-way bill operations, multiple entities, notice reply |
| Manufacturer / Hospital | From ₹6,999 | Custom scope after discovery: GSTIN count, transaction volume, TDS/payroll/ROC needs and reporting | Never auto-include payroll, ROC, audit, legal retainers or unlimited transactions |

For every plan, define invoice counting before onboarding. Count sales and purchase invoices together for the service cap; if turnover, GSTIN count or complexity creates materially higher work, quote the higher band. Do not silently downgrade or overcharge: get written client approval before moving a customer to another band.

## 5. Direct-cost and margin gate

Use contribution margin after variable fulfilment costs, not gross revenue, to decide whether a plan is viable.

**Contribution = customer fee − professional/associate payout − payment charges − variable support/operations cost.**

Target at least **35% contribution** after these variable costs for standard recurring plans. A pilot may be approved at 25–34% only with a documented learning objective and a review date. Below 25%, re-scope or reprice; do not scale.

Maximum combined variable-cost budget at 35% contribution:

| Price | Maximum variable costs |
|---:|---:|
| ₹699 | ₹454 |
| ₹999 | ₹649 |
| ₹1,499 | ₹974 |
| ₹1,999 | ₹1,299 |
| ₹3,999 | ₹2,599 |
| ₹6,999 | ₹4,549 |

These are ceilings, not estimates of actual professional payout. Before launch, enter actual CA/GST practitioner/associate rates, average minutes per customer, support time, payment charges and rework rate. Include onboarding and acquisition cost in a separate payback analysis. Do not claim profitability until measured.

## 6. Pilot and operational controls

1. Pilot with 10–20 local shopkeepers/retailers before opening India-wide recurring plans.
2. Onboard only after a written checklist: entity type, GSTIN count, filing frequency, turnover band, sales/purchase invoice count, accounting software, TDS/payroll/ROC requirements and backlog status.
3. Collect source records through secure upload; never ask clients to share GST portal passwords or OTPs. Client authorises the practitioner through the official GST portal where required.
4. Use monthly advance billing for recurring plans. Do not offer unlimited work, unlimited revisions or automatic annual renewal.
5. Initial term: month-to-month pilot; any minimum term, annual discount or cancellation fee needs separate approval and clear disclosure.
6. Create a service completion checklist: return ARN, filed copy, tax payable/challan status, reconciliation notes (if included), unresolved exceptions and next due date.
7. Escalate notices, audits, refunds, appeals, historic reconciliation and multi-GSTIN matters to a scope review before accepting payment.
8. Track each customer's gross fee, direct delivery cost, minutes, rework, on-time filing rate, renewal and contribution margin monthly.

## 7. Distribution strategy

- Use the existing GST Suvidha Kendra relationship as a local trust and acquisition channel, subject to the franchise agreement and applicable rules.
- Start with nearby retailers and wholesalers; offer a no-cost 10-minute eligibility/scope conversation, not free filing or free professional work.
- Build referral relationships with local accountants, bookkeepers, trade associations and market associations.
- The website catalogue should remain the discovery layer; CRM should capture business type, GSTIN count, invoice band, requested service and follow-up status.
- Do not bundle legal representation with GST packages. Offer legal assistance separately, with clear scope and professional engagement.

## 8. Release decision checklist

Do not publish plan prices or enable recurring checkout until all are true:

- [ ] Actual professional and associate cost sheet completed
- [ ] Service limits, deliverables and exclusions approved
- [ ] GST treatment of ILS fees confirmed by the finance/accounting owner
- [ ] Client engagement terms, privacy/data handling and cancellation terms approved
- [ ] CRM plan lead fields and assignment workflow verified
- [ ] Billing, renewal, pause, cancellation and refund flows tested
- [ ] Pilot contribution margin measured for at least one complete billing cycle
- [ ] Explicit owner approval for public pricing and Production release

**Current recommendation:** retain individual services as one-time purchases. Keep business plans enquiry-led with no recurring charge until the pilot proves the workload and margin assumptions.
