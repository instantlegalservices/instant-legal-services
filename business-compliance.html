How the service works

A clear process, with scope and charges explained before you commit.

1. Tell us what you need

Share your business type, service and relevant deadline. Do not send passwords or OTPs.

2. Confirm the scope

We identify the documents, work involved and whether specialist professional input is needed.

3. Review the fee

ILS service charges are shown separately from government fees, taxes or statutory payments.

4. Start the service

Work begins after your confirmation and any required payment or documents.

Important: Instant Legal Services is an independent assistance platform, not a government department. Government registrations, filings, statutory payments and final decisions remain with the relevant authority. ILS charges, if any, are separate and will be disclosed before payment. Legal advice, representation and reserved professional work are handled by an appropriately qualified independent professional. Never share portal passwords, OTPs, PINs or payment credentials.
