How to Check Court Case Status in India

For current case status, use the official eCourts service rather than relying on an unofficial summary. Depending on the court and service, you may use information such as a CNR, case number, party details or other available search fields.

Basic steps

  1. Open the official eCourts service.
  2. Select the relevant court/service and enter the available case identifier or search information.
  3. Review the returned case status, dates and orders shown by the official service.
  4. For a legal interpretation of an order or procedural position, verify the actual order/judgment and applicable law.
