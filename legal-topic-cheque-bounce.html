Cheque Bounce / Section 138: Research Guide

Cheque dishonour matters are procedure-sensitive. Before relying on a deadline, notice requirement or case proposition, verify the current statutory text and applicable judgments.

Research checklist

  • Verify the current Negotiable Instruments Act provision and applicable amendments.
  • Check the dates and procedural sequence in the specific matter.
  • Review the jurisdictional and evidentiary issues relevant to the complaint.
  • Confirm current Supreme Court and High Court precedent.
